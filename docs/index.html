This APK needs sideloading. Installing it on your phone alone won’t add it to your watch.

Step-by-step pairing guide ↗

  1. Download & prepare.

    Save the APK and install Wear Installer 2 on your Android phone.

  2. Pair over Wi-Fi.

    Use the same Wi-Fi network. Enable Developer options, ADB debugging and Wireless debugging on the watch. Follow the linked guide to pair with its code and pairing port.

  3. Install the APK.

    Use the watch’s connection port—not its pairing port. In Wear Installer 2, choose Custom APK, select the file and install.

  4. Select & customize.

    Add Edge Forecast from your watch-face picker. Customize its style, trail and complications. Turn off ADB and Wireless debugging afterward.

  5. Add phone battery (optional).

    Install and open Phone Battery Complication by amoledwatchfaces on both your Android phone and Wear OS watch. In Edge Forecast’s editor, choose Phone Battery for the phone-battery slot.

HELP

A few answers.

Why might weather or phone battery be missing?

Weather needs the watch’s weather service and permissions. Phone battery needs the companion setup above. Tapping weather targets Samsung Weather, which must be installed.

Updates or issues?

Updates are manual downloads from this page. Report an issue on GitHub with your watch model and software version. Don’t include pairing codes or private details.

Does this page track me?

GitHub provides the download count and may log visits and download requests. No additional analytics or ad service is used; see its privacy policy. Watch services and companion apps have their own policies.
